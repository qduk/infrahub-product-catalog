Framework requirement verdicts

Proposed verdicts for every coverage claim in the Network Automation Product Catalog, against the MUST, SHOULD and MAY requirements of the Network Automation Framework. Nothing is loaded until approved.

A claim is full only when every MUST and SHOULD requirement of its block is met. The two framework-wide SHOULD requirements are shown but do not count. Partly met, not met and not shown by any source all count against full. MAY requirements are shown but do not change the extent. The Executor and Collector MUSTs use your looser reading: at least one of the listed write interfaces, and live reads plus at least one kind of metrics, logs, flows, packets or traces.
