# Contract: Graph View

| Interaction | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| Drag the background, scroll, pinch | Pan and zoom |
| Fit control | Whole graph in view |
| Click a product | Highlight it, its edges and blocks; fade the rest; open details |
| Click a block | Highlight it and its products; fade the rest |
| Click the background or press Escape | Clear the highlight; close details |
| Status and coverage filters | Hide product nodes and edges that do not match |
| Click a block's "show only" control, or `?block=` in the page address | Show only that block and its products |
